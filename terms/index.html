Eligibility

Gulper is a drink-tracking app and is only intended for use by people who have reached the legal drinking age in their country. By using Gulper, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

Not medical or safety advice

Gulper's blood alcohol content (BAC) estimates are calculated from the information you provide and are approximate — they are not a medical device and are not a precise or reliable measurement of your actual BAC. Many factors Gulper can't account for affect real BAC. This applies equally to every feature built on those estimates, including your BAC progression over an evening, your time until sober, your estimated tolerance, and any figure shown on a friend's profile. Never use Gulper's estimate to decide whether it's safe to drive, operate machinery, or engage in any activity requiring sobriety. Always follow local law and use your own judgment, and use a certified breathalyzer or medical testing if you need an accurate reading.

AI-powered features

Scan Drink uses Anthropic's AI to identify drinks from photos you submit. Results are estimates and may be inaccurate — always double-check the volume and ABV before relying on a scanned entry. See our Privacy Policy for how your photos are handled.

Barcode scanning and the drink database

Barcode scanning looks products up in Open Food Facts, a public database Gulper doesn't control and can't guarantee. Entries there are sometimes incomplete or wrong. Where a product's alcohol content is missing, Gulper fills in a typical value for that kind of drink so the entry is usable — this is an assumption, not a measurement, and the app flags the entry for you to check. The same applies to Gulper's own built-in drink database, whose sizes and ABVs are typical values for each product. Always confirm the volume and ABV before saving a drink; an incorrect figure will make every estimate built on it wrong.

Product details and photos found by barcode come from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org): the data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL), and the photos under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence.

Gulper+ can come with a free trial, which Apple gives once per Apple Account. When it does, the app shows how long it lasts and what you'll pay after it before you subscribe. Apple takes the first payment when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. If you cancel during the trial, Gulper+ stays until the trial ends and you aren't charged.

Gulper+ currently adds BAC progression, four additional insights (the weekly review, estimated spending, estimated calories and lifetime statistics), unlimited drink and barcode scans (the free plan allows five per day), three months of calendar history instead of one, membership of up to five groups instead of two, and the Subscriber achievement, a badge only Gulper+ members can earn. Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel it. Because Apple handles the billing, only you can cancel it — go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device.
