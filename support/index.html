How do I cancel Gulper+?

Apple bills Gulper+, so you cancel it with Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Gulper → Cancel Subscription. In Gulper, Profile → Manage Subscription → Manage subscription takes you to the same place.

Your Gulper+ features stay until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting your Gulper account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple takes every Gulper+ payment, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Gulper+, but it isn't unlocked

Make sure you're signed in to the Gulper account you bought it with, then go to Profile → Manage Subscription → Restore purchases. That's also what to do on a new phone. If it's still locked, email us.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap Report or Block. Only the Gulper team sees reports, and the person you report isn't notified. We review every report.

Blocking someone removes them as a friend and stops them sending you friend requests or adding you to a group. You can see and unblock people under Profile → Blocked Users.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account Actions → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Cancel Gulper+ first: deleting your account doesn't stop Apple charging you.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email supportgulper@gmail.com, ideally from the address you signed up with, and we'll send it to you. You can also ask us to correct or delete it.

How accurate is the BAC estimate?

It's an estimate worked out from what you log, your body details and how full your stomach is, not a measurement. Never use it to decide whether you can drive. If you need a real reading, use a certified breathalyzer.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.
